RadioMusic32 · SD card samples

Sample Manager

Lay out the card's 16 banks. Add samples, or open an existing card folder, then preview, reorder and move them between voices and banks. Nothing is converted until you download the ZIP.

How the module reads the card. Bank folders A–P, each with 1/ (voice 1, sampler) and 2/ (voice 2, chromatic), each holding up to 16 files named 01.wav–16.wav.

  • Pot 1 picks the sample number for both voices at once. If a voice has fewer samples than the pot position, it plays its last one. That's why the order here matters.
  • Files are written as mono 16-bit 44.1 kHz WAV, the module's own rate, so voice 1 needs no rate conversion. Use full-bandwidth sources: the module's S950 mode adds the 12-bit tone and darkening, without aliasing.

Keys and tips

Move: drag samples to reorder them, drop them on the other voice, or drop them on a bank tab to move them into that bank. Hold ⌥ (Alt) when dropping to copy instead. On a phone, select and use Move to….

Select: click a row; SHIFT + click a range; ⌘ (Ctrl) + click to add or remove one; ⌘A for the whole voice.

Edit: ⌘X cut · ⌘C copy · ⌘V paste after the selection (switch bank first to move between banks) · ⌥↑ ⌥↓ move up and down · ⌫ remove · ⌘Z undo · SHIFT ⌘Z redo · SPACE play / stop · [ ] previous / next bank.

Adding: drop audio files from your computer straight onto a voice, at the position you want. A full voice (16) refuses more.

Existing cards: Open card folder… reads the A/1 … P/2 folders of a card or a copy of one. Copy the unzipped download back to the card's root, replacing the bank folders.
